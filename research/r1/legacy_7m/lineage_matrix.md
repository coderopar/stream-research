## Mechanism-family and source map

- Existing `FAM-LIQUIDITY-RESILIENCE`: withdrawal, durable queue loss, opposing refill breadth/near-restoration, supporting refill (`LEG7M-01/02/04/05`).
- Existing `FAM-IMPACT-EFFICIENCY-ABSORPTION`: replacement penetration (`LEG7M-06`), explicitly including its null/negative historical result.
- Existing `FAM-METAORDER-FLOW-PERSISTENCE`: legacy trade-size concentration (`LEG7M-07`), D/V failure preserved.
- Existing `FAM-CROSS-VENUE-INFORMATION-DIFFUSION`: spot flow/price and direct OKX BBO/price (`LEG7M-09/10`), including failed pre-outcome matching.
- New positioning/forced-flow, basis, funding, options IV and gamma-geometry families preserve genuinely different conceptual mechanisms (`LEG7M-07/08/11A/11B/11C/12A`) rather than outcome-derived family splits.

### What chronology cannot establish

A phase-local file can report that *its* matching or exposure was frozen before *its* outcome load, but the archive does not establish clean independence of the overall program's historical threshold, horizon, metric, candidate-selection and feature-family decisions. Nor does the archived 7M output demonstrate an executable net-return strategy under realistic costs. The R0 causal-clock repair and dependence audit constrain all future interpretation.
